About this view

Photo selection changes visibility only. Poses come from the full saved
reconstruction. Separate coordinate frames have unknown relative placement.
Room hypotheses overlay the selected reconstruction using saved photo IDs.


Changing reconstruction options selects the corresponding saved geometry and poses. The
largest group is initially selected; subsequent selections are remembered per
combination. Offscreen 3D views release resources and restore their view on return.


Where available, the VLM switch selects a saved connection-review experiment. No model runs in the
browser. Components and poses were recomputed after edge removal; original clouds
and K were retained. The initial photo selection is preserved. Same-room and doorway
relations do not create a shared coordinate frame. Unknown answers are not support.


Cycles check path consistency; this viewer does not optimize poses. Subgroup
colors describe visible graph connectivity, not geometric accuracy.


Connection recovery uses saved scale-aware fits and positive VLM landmarks in
both image orders. Each original component receives one rotation, translation and
scale, preserving its internal relative poses. Units follow the anchor model;
metric accuracy of the scale corrections is not established.


The room source compares pairwise hypotheses and saved all-photo Qwen answers.
Orders and repeats remain separate. Semantic selection adds no geometric edges
and changes no poses. Overlapping hypotheses are alternatives, not a room count.
Disconnected parts are arranged for inspection, with unknown physical placement.


Coordinates use relative model units. Lengths in meters require a separate scale estimate. GeoCalib applies one rotation per component. Without it, world
vertical is unknown. Each model's XYZ and K are used together.


All saved loads the complete display cloud. Some reports export a preview sample.
Fused surfels are stored once and can appear through multiple supporting photos.
Detail caps each stored surfel buffer before applying the photo selection.
Lower detail reduces the rendered count. Selection preserves saved geometry and metrics.


Measure distance: click A, then B to add a ruler. Points/Both snap to saved cloud vertices; Patches uses positions inside visible triangles. Labels retain source photos. Select a ruler to see its 3D length, horizontal distance and vertical difference. Drag A/B to adjust, or click an endpoint then choose a new location. Escape cancels an unfinished pick or edit; completed rulers remain until deleted. Measurements cannot cross disconnected inspection parts. Changing selected geometry, representation or coordinate transforms clears incompatible rulers.


Distances use the selected reconstruction's units and include reconstruction and alignment errors. Horizontal and vertical components follow the displayed axes. A cross-section shows the band selected by its width control.


Drag to orbit, Shift/right-drag to pan, scroll/pinch to zoom, double-click a surface to focus. The orientation widget and View presets select axis-aligned or isometric views. Orthographic projection removes perspective size changes. Cameras highlight on hover and open the source image on click/tap; measurement mode takes priority.


Explore opens one aligned group in perspective at a predicted photo position. Drag to look; WASD/arrows or the mobile movement pad moves horizontally, Q/E or the height buttons moves vertically. Scroll moves through the scene, independently of orbit zoom. Returning to Inspect restores the previous camera. No collision detection or measured floor height is implied. Separate components are explored separately; photo jumps are viewpoint changes, not verified walkable paths.


On mobile, Adjust with scene visible collapses settings to a bottom strip for slice position/width, cutaway height or point size. The strip reserves its own space, leaving the canvas unobscured. Rendering diagnostics stay outside enlarged mode.


Cutaway and cross-section clip reconstructed surfaces consistently, including picking. Rulers and camera annotations remain visible. Display controls preserve saved geometry and metrics. Cross-section position is relative to each group's bounds; width is in its model units.


Saved views live in this browser. A view link encodes settings and rulers in its fragment, without cloud files or a server write. Restore requires the same scene data. Sharing a full-detail view downloads geometry only when that link is opened; switching view controls reuses loaded assets.
