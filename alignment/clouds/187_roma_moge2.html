About this view

Photo selection changes visibility only. Poses use the full graph and remain fixed when the visible subgraph splits. Originally disconnected components have separate frames with unknown relative placement. A connected component is not a room.

Changing matcher or depth selects that result's edges, groups and poses. The largest group is initially selected; subsequent selections are remembered per combination. Offscreen 3D views release resources and restore their view on return.

The VLM switch selects a saved connection-review experiment. No model runs in the browser. Components and poses were recomputed after edge removal; original clouds and K were retained. The initial photo selection is preserved. Same-room and doorway relations do not create a shared coordinate frame. Unknown answers are not support.

Cycles check path consistency; this viewer does not optimize poses. Subgroup colors describe visible graph connectivity, not geometric accuracy.

Connection recovery uses saved scale-aware fits and positive VLM landmarks in both image orders. Each original component receives one rotation, translation and scale, preserving its internal relative poses. Units follow the anchor model; metric accuracy of the scale corrections is not established.

The room source compares pairwise hypotheses and saved all-photo Qwen answers. Orders and repeats remain separate. Semantic selection adds no geometric edges and changes no poses. Overlapping hypotheses are alternatives, not a room count. Disconnected parts are arranged for inspection, with unknown physical placement.

The model predicts meters; zero height is at the camera, not the floor. GeoCalib applies one rotation per component. Without it, world vertical is unknown. Each model's XYZ and K are used together.

Points are nested uniform pixel samples; detailed levels load on demand. Toggling a photo does not change the samples of other sources.

Planar patches approximate predicted geometry. Surfaces from different photos are not fused, so duplicate walls remain. Only fully supported grid cells are rendered; boundary strips and nonplanar regions can be missing. Vertex colors lose fine image detail. Use Both to compare patch support with the original cloud.
